## Common Whirlpool problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Top-load washer won’t spin

Many newer top-loaders lock the lid before they spin. A worn lid lock, a broken lid strike or water left in the drum will stop the cycle. These are common, replaceable parts.

### Dryer runs but no heat

Dryers protect themselves with a thermal fuse that opens when the dryer overheats, usually because the vent is restricted. We replace the failed part and check the vent so it does not happen again.

### Refrigerator ice maker stopped

The usual causes are a water inlet valve, a frozen fill tube, an old filter or the ice maker itself. The technician tests each before ordering anything.

### Dishwasher leaving water behind

Most Whirlpool-family dishwashers have a removable filter under the lower spray arm. A clogged filter is the first check, then the drain hose and pump.

### Do you also repair Maytag, KitchenAid and Amana?

Yes. Maytag, KitchenAid, JennAir and Amana are Whirlpool Corporation brands, and many of their parts are shared. Tell us the brand and model number when you book.

### Are you an authorized Whirlpool service center?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service. We are not affiliated with or authorized by Whirlpool. We repair Whirlpool appliances that are out of warranty or where you prefer a local independent technician.

### My Whirlpool is still under warranty. Should I call you?

Contact Whirlpool first. Independent repair may not be covered by the manufacturer’s warranty. If the warranty has expired, call us.

### What does the error code on my Whirlpool mean?

Codes point to a system, such as draining, the door lock or a sensor, and their meaning varies by model. Write the code down, or take a photo, and give it to us when you call.

### Can you repair my Whirlpool the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Many common Whirlpool parts can be replaced on the first visit. Some model-specific parts need to be ordered.

### How much does Whirlpool appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number ready.
