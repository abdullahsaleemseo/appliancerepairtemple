## What we see in Morgan's Point Resort.

### Same ZIP as Belton

Many addresses here use 76513 and some show Belton as the mailing city. When you book, tell us the street and that you are in Morgan's Point Resort. It helps the technician plan the route and find your home on the first try.

### Garage fridges and lake freezers

Lake living tends to mean a second refrigerator for drinks and a chest freezer for fish and game, often in a garage that runs hot in summer. Keep the coils clean, leave a few inches of space around the unit and avoid stacking boxes on top of a chest freezer.

### Older lake houses

Some homes near the water date back decades, with small kitchens, original hookups and long dryer vent runs. On laundry calls the technician checks the vent and the water connections too, because they cause many repeat failures.

### Manufactured homes

Manufactured homes often have apartment-size ranges, compact refrigerators and tight laundry closets with little room behind the machines. Let us know the layout when you book so the technician can plan for the space.

## Before you call try these first.

Power can flicker during storms. If an appliance stopped right after the power blinked, check the breaker and any GFCI outlet in the garage or kitchen first. Then unplug the appliance for a minute and plug it back in.

If a garage refrigerator is not cooling, look at where it sits. In a closed garage in July, many standard refrigerators struggle. Pull it a few inches from the wall and check that the condenser fan is running. If it still runs warm, or it clicks on and off every few minutes, book a visit.

## Repairs we handle in Morgan's Point Resort.

Refrigerators and freezers lead the list in summer: warm fresh food sections, frost on the freezer wall, ice makers that stop and chest freezers that will not start. Start relays, fan motors and defrost parts are frequent fixes.

Washers that will not drain or spin and dryers that run without heat come next. On every dryer call we check the vent, since a long or clogged vent is one of the most common reasons a dryer stops heating.

Dishwashers that leave dishes dirty or water in the tub, and ovens with a burned bake element or a temperature that has drifted, round out the list.
