## Before you send it.

### Does sending this form book an appointment?

No. It requests a callback. The appointment is set once we confirm a time with you by phone.

### What if I do not know the brand or model?

Choose your best guess or leave it blank. A plain description of what the appliance is doing is enough to get started.

### I sent a request after hours. When will I hear back?

Our hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Requests sent outside those hours are followed up once we are open.

### Can I get a price before the visit?

The cost depends on the appliance, the model and the part that failed, so we do not quote prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs.
