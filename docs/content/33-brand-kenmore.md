## Kenmore repair in Temple.

Kenmore is a little different from other brands. For decades Kenmore appliances were sold through Sears but built by other manufacturers, including Whirlpool, LG, Frigidaire and others. That means two Kenmore refrigerators can have completely different parts inside, depending on who made them.

The good news is that the model number tells us who built it. The digits before the dot point to the manufacturer, and the full number tells us exactly which parts fit. Many Kenmore appliances in Temple homes are older units that still run well, and a lot of them are worth repairing.

## Common Kenmore problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Washer will not spin or drain

On Whirlpool-built Kenmore top-loaders, a worn lid switch or lid lock, a broken belt, a failed drive coupler or a clogged pump are common. On LG-built front-loaders, the drain filter and door lock come first. The model number tells us which design you have.

### Dryer runs but no heat

A heating element, thermal fuse or thermostat on electric dryers, an igniter or gas valve coils on gas dryers. Older dryers often have long vent runs packed with lint, so airflow is checked on every call.

### Refrigerator or freezer running warm

Dusty condenser coils, a failed fan, a defrost problem or a start relay are the usual causes, especially on older units in garages. These are reasonable repairs on most models.

### Parts for older models

Some older Kenmore parts have been discontinued. Because Kenmore shared designs with its manufacturers, a compatible part can often be found through the original builder. The technician will tell you plainly if a part is no longer available.

### Are you affiliated with Kenmore or Sears?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Kenmore, Sears or any manufacturer that built Kenmore appliances.

### Who made my Kenmore appliance?

The digits before the dot in the model number identify the manufacturer. For example, 110 and 106 point to Whirlpool and 795 points to LG. Give us the full model number and we will look it up.

### Is my old Kenmore worth repairing?

Often, yes. Many older Kenmore washers, dryers and refrigerators use simple, durable parts. If a key part has been discontinued or the repair is major on a very old unit, the technician will tell you.

### Can you still get parts for older Kenmore models?

Many are still available, sometimes under the original manufacturer’s part number. Some have been discontinued. We check before ordering and tell you what we find.

### Can you repair my Kenmore the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Some parts are model-specific and may need to be ordered.
