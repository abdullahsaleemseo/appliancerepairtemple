Local notes

## What we see in Killeen.

### PCS moves and move-in surprises

Appliances that sit empty between tenants or get unplugged for a move often come back with stuck drain pumps, dried-out door seals or a refrigerator that will not cool after it is restarted. If you just moved in, give a fridge a full day to settle before you call, and report any problem to your landlord in writing.

### Landlords and property managers

Killeen has a large rental market, and we work with owners and managers as well as tenants. Tell us who approves the repair, who handles payment and who will let the technician in. That keeps the visit from stalling at the door.

### Older additions on the north side

Homes in north and northwest Killeen tend to be older. Laundry closets can be tight, dryer vent runs long, and shutoff valves original to the house. On laundry calls the technician checks the vent and the water hookups too, since they cause a lot of repeat failures.

### Newer homes south of I-14

The area south of I-14 between Stan Schlueter Loop and Chaparral Road holds much of Killeen’s newest building. Those homes often came with French door refrigerators, front-load laundry and electronic controls that show error codes. Write the code down and check the factory warranty before you call.

## What we fix in Killeen homes.

Refrigerators and freezers come first in summer. A dusty condenser coil, a failed fan motor or a defrost problem can turn a working fridge warm in a day. If the freezer is cold but the fresh food side is warm, that pattern usually points to airflow or defrost, both common repairs.

Laundry is close behind. Busy households wear out dryer belts, rollers and heating parts faster, and washers that will not drain usually have a clogged pump or a worn lid lock. We check airflow on every dryer call, because a restricted vent is often why the heating part failed.

Dishwashers that leave dishes dirty or water in the tub often have a blocked filter, a worn pump or scale in the spray arms. Ovens and ranges round out the list with burned bake elements, weak igniters and temperature sensors that have drifted.

## Renting in Killeen? Check this first.

Many leases put landlord-supplied appliances on the landlord. Before you book a repair yourself, read your lease and contact your landlord or property manager. If they ask you to arrange the visit, let us know who is approving the work so we can confirm it before the technician starts.

If you live in on-post housing at Fort Hood, appliance problems go through your housing office and its maintenance contractor, not an outside repair company. Off-post homes and rentals in Killeen are the ones we serve.

## How scheduling works for Killeen.

Call or send a request with your address, the appliance, the brand and what it is doing. We confirm the details by phone and set a visit window. We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Our typical response is about 60 minutes during business hours. That is a typical figure, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand, not a guaranteed arrival time. Because Killeen sits at the west end of our routes, an early call gives you a better shot at a same-day visit.

Before the visit, find the model number, usually on a label inside the door, under the lid or on the frame, and note any error code. Clear a path to the appliance and, if you can, empty the washer or dishwasher.

## Repair in Killeen: questions.

### Which parts of Killeen do you cover?

We serve homes in Killeen’s 76541, 76542, 76543 and 76549 ZIP codes. If your address is on the edge of town or outside city limits, give it to us and we will confirm.

### Can you come to Killeen the same day?

Sometimes. Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Killeen is at the west end of our routes, so calling early helps.

### Do you repair appliances in on-post housing at Fort Hood?

No. On-post housing has its own maintenance process. Contact your housing office, since its contractor handles appliance repair there. We serve off-post homes and rentals in Killeen.

### I rent my home. Who pays for the repair?

That depends on your lease. Most leases make the landlord responsible for appliances they provide. Check with your landlord or property manager before booking.

### Can my property manager schedule the repair?

Yes. We can work with the manager directly. We need to know who approves the repair and who will let the technician in.

### My fridge stopped cooling after we moved. Is it broken?

Not always. A refrigerator that was moved or unplugged can take up to a day to reach temperature. If it is still warm after that, or it runs nonstop, call us.

### Which appliances do you repair in Killeen?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range microwaves, across the major brands.

### How much does appliance repair in Killeen cost?
