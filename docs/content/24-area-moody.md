## Cold snaps are hard on appliances too.

Central Texas freezes do not last long, but they catch appliances off guard. Water lines to refrigerator ice makers and washer hoses in unheated laundry rooms or garages can freeze and split. After a hard freeze, check behind the washer and the refrigerator for drips before you run them.

Garage refrigerators and freezers can also act up in the cold. Many standard refrigerators are not built to run in a garage that drops near freezing, and the fresh food side may warm up while the freezer thaws. If yours only misbehaves on cold nights, that is useful information for the technician.

## Ice maker stopped? Check these first.

Make sure the ice maker is switched on and the shutoff arm or sensor is not stuck in the full position. Check that the freezer is cold enough, usually around 0°F, since many ice makers stop below a certain temperature. If you recently changed the water filter, reseat it, as a filter that is not fully locked in can block water flow.

If ice is still not forming, the problem is often a failed water inlet valve, a frozen fill tube or the ice maker assembly itself. Those are common repairs, so book a visit with the model number ready.

### Moody is in McLennan County. Do you really come out from Temple?

### My washer hose split after a freeze. What should I do?

Shut off the water supply valves behind the washer, unplug it and dry the area. Then call us. Do not run the washer until the hose and connections have been checked.

### My ice maker stopped. Is it worth repairing?

Usually. Inlet valves, fill tubes and ice maker assemblies are common, reasonable repairs. Check that the ice maker is on and the filter is seated first.

### Do you repair freezers and fridges in shops and barns?

Yes. Make sure the technician can reach the back of the unit and the outlet, and let us know about any gates.

### Which appliances do you repair in Moody?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range microwaves, across the major brands.

### What happens if a part has to be ordered?

Many common parts can be replaced on the first visit. Some are model-specific and have to be ordered. The technician will tell you if that is the case and what happens next.

### How much does appliance repair in Moody cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the brand and model number ready.
