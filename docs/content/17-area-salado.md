## What we see in Salado.

### Freezers and fridges in barns and shops

Spare refrigerators and chest freezers out in a barn or shop deal with heat, dust and the occasional mouse nest in the condenser area. Keep a few inches of clearance around the unit, vacuum the coils a couple of times a year and keep the floor around it clear.

### Historic homes in the village

Salado has nineteen sites on the National Register of Historic Places, and some of the older homes near the village center have tight kitchens and older wiring. If the technician sees anything electrical that should go to an electrician, you will be told plainly.

### Long driveways and gates

For homes on acreage, tell us about gates, gate codes, dogs and where to park when you book. If the appliance is in an outbuilding, let us know which one. It saves time on arrival.

### Power blips and surges

Rural power lines see more outages and flickers during storms. If an appliance stopped after the power went out, check the breaker and any GFCI outlet first, then unplug it for a minute and plug it back in before you call.

## Is that barn freezer worth fixing?

Often, yes. Many freezer failures come down to a start relay, a thermostat or a fan motor, and those are reasonable repairs. If the freezer hums, clicks and shuts off every few minutes, that pattern often points to the start relay or the compressor.

A failed compressor on an older, small chest freezer is usually a different story, and the technician will tell you when a repair costs too much relative to the unit. While you wait, keep the lid closed. A full chest freezer holds its temperature longer than a half-empty one.

## Repairs we handle around Salado.

Refrigerators that run but do not cool, frost on the freezer wall, ice makers that quit and water leaking under the crisper drawers are the most common kitchen calls. Fan motors, defrost parts and water valves are frequent fixes.

Washers that will not drain or spin and dryers that run without heat come next. On every dryer call we check the vent, since a long or crushed vent run is one of the most common reasons a dryer stops heating.

Dishwashers that leave a film or do not drain, and ovens with a burned bake element or a temperature that has drifted, round out the list.

### Do you come out to properties outside the village?

### Can you come to Salado the same day?

### My chest freezer in the barn stopped. Is it worth repairing?

If it is a relay, thermostat or fan, usually yes. A failed compressor on an older, small chest freezer is usually not worth repairing. The technician will tell you which it is.

### Do you repair propane ranges and dryers?

Mention propane when you book. We will confirm whether we can take the job before scheduling the visit.

### My appliance stopped after a storm. What should I check?

Check the breaker and any GFCI outlet, then unplug the appliance for a minute and plug it back in. If it still does not work, call us.

### Which appliances do you repair in Salado?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range microwaves, across the major brands.

### What happens if a part has to be ordered?
