## Common Speed Queen problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Top-load washer will not drain or spin

A clogged drain pump or hose, a worn lid lock or lid switch, or a worn belt are the common causes. Something small like a sock in the pump can stop a cycle. The technician checks the drain path and lock before looking at the motor or transmission.

### Front-load washer stops or will not unlock

A clogged drain pump, a door lock that is not engaging, or an unbalanced load the machine will not spin. Front-loaders also need the door gasket wiped and the door left open between loads to prevent odors.

### Loud washer on spin

A loud thump usually means an unbalanced load or worn suspension parts. A roar or grinding that gets louder with speed often points to the tub bearings. Stop using it if it starts walking across the floor.

### Dryer runs but no heat

A heating element, thermal fuse or high-limit thermostat on electric models, or an igniter or gas valve coils on gas models. A clogged vent is often the root cause, so airflow is checked on every dryer call.

### Are you an authorized Speed Queen service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Speed Queen or Alliance Laundry Systems. If your machine is under Speed Queen’s warranty, contact Speed Queen first.

### Is my Speed Queen still under warranty?

Possibly. Many recent Speed Queen washers and dryers carry long warranties, and the exact coverage depends on the model and serial number. Check your paperwork or contact Speed Queen before booking a paid repair.

### Is an older Speed Queen worth repairing?

Usually, yes. These machines are built to last, and pumps, lid locks, belts, switches and heating parts are reasonable repairs. Tub bearings or a transmission on a very old unit are the main cases where it gets closer.

### Do you repair commercial or coin-operated Speed Queen machines?

Our work is residential. If your machine is in a shared laundry room or used commercially, tell us when you call and we will let you know whether we can help.
