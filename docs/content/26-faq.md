# Temple Appliance Repair FAQ, answered plainly.

These are the questions people in Temple and the nearby towns ask us most, from booking a visit to deciding whether an old appliance is worth fixing. If yours is not here, call and ask.

## Booking and scheduling

### How do I schedule a repair?

Call during business hours, or send the request form with your appliance, what it is doing and your ZIP code. We call back to confirm the details and set a visit window.

### Does sending the form book an appointment?

No. The form requests a callback. The appointment is set once we confirm a time with you by phone.

### Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Calling earlier in the day gives you a better shot at a same-day visit.

### How quickly do you respond?

Our typical response is about 60 minutes during business hours. That is a typical figure, not a guaranteed arrival time, and it depends on availability, your location and demand that day.

### What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

### Do I need to be home for the visit?

An adult needs to be there to let the technician in and approve the repair. A landlord or property manager can approve a repair for a tenant if that is arranged when booking.

## The visit and the diagnosis

### What happens when the technician arrives?

The technician asks what you have noticed, tests the parts most likely to have failed and explains what is wrong and what the repair involves. Nothing is replaced until you say yes.

### What should I do before the technician arrives?

Find the model number, usually on a label inside the door, under the lid or on the frame. Write down any error code, clear a path to the appliance and, if you can, empty the washer or dishwasher.

### Can you tell me what is wrong over the phone?

We can talk through the symptoms and what they often point to, but the cause is confirmed on site. The same symptom can come from different parts on different models.

### Do technicians carry parts?

Many common parts can be replaced on the first visit. Model-specific parts sometimes have to be ordered, and the technician will tell you if that is the case and what happens next.

### Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed and insured.

## Repair or replace

### Is my appliance worth repairing?

Usually, if it is not near the end of its normal life and the failed part is a common one like a pump, fan, relay, belt or heating element. A common rule of thumb: if the repair would cost more than half of a comparable new appliance and the unit is getting old, replacement deserves a serious look.

### How long do appliances usually last?

As a rough guide, refrigerators about 10 to 15 years, washers and dryers about 10 to 13, dishwashers about 9 to 12 and ranges about 13 to 15. Use and care move those numbers a lot.

### Will you tell me if it is not worth fixing?

Yes. If a repair does not make sense for your appliance, the technician will say so, and the decision is yours.

## Appliances, brands and areas

### Which appliances do you repair?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range or built-in microwaves. If you have something else, call and ask.

### Which brands do you work on?

The major household brands, including Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Frigidaire, Maytag, KitchenAid, Bosch, Kenmore, Electrolux, Amana and Speed Queen. Tell us the brand and model when you book.

### Are you an authorized service center for any brand?

No. We are an independent repair service, not affiliated with or authorized by any manufacturer.

### Do you repair gas appliances?

Tell us it is a gas range, oven or dryer when you book so we can confirm before the visit. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas provider or 911 from outside first.

### Which areas do you serve?

Temple (76501, 76502, 76503, 76504 and 76508) plus Belton, Killeen, Harker Heights, Nolanville, Morgan’s Point Resort, Salado, Troy, Little River-Academy, Rogers, Holland and Moody. Give us your address and we will confirm.

## Payment and warranty

### Why are there no prices on the website?

The cost depends on the appliance, the model and the part that failed, so a number on a web page would only be a guess. Call to discuss your repair needs with the brand and model number ready.

### What payment options do you offer?

Flexible payment options are available. Ask about them when you call.

### My appliance is still under warranty. Should I call you?

Check with the manufacturer or your extended plan first, since independent repair may not be covered by a factory warranty. If coverage has run out or you are not sure, call us with the model number.

### I rent my home. Who pays for the repair?

That depends on your lease. Many leases make the landlord responsible for the appliances they supply, so check with your landlord or property manager before booking.

## Safety first

### When should I stop using an appliance right away?

If you see sparks, smell burning plastic or hot wiring, find scorch marks on a cord or outlet, or see water pooling near an outlet. Unplug it or switch off the breaker and call us.

### My dryer smells hot. Is that dangerous?

It can be. Lint buildup is a leading cause of dryer fires. Stop the dryer, clean the lint screen and check that the outside vent flap opens when it runs. If the smell continues, do not use it until it has been checked.
