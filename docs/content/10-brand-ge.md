## Common GE problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Gas oven slow to heat or not lighting

On gas ranges, a weak hot surface igniter is the most common cause. It can glow and still fail to open the gas valve. The technician tests it with a meter before looking at the valve or the board.

### Refrigerator warm, freezer still cold

Usually a frosted evaporator coil from a defrost problem, or an evaporator fan that stopped. Either way, cold air is not reaching the fresh-food side.

### Dishwasher not draining

Check the filter, the drain hose loop under the sink and the disposal connection first. If those are clear, the drain pump is the likely cause.

### Electric burner or element not heating

A burned element, a loose or burned terminal, or a failed infinite switch. All are standard parts on most GE ranges.

### Do you work on GE Profile and Café appliances?

Yes. Tell us the exact model when you book, since GE Profile, Café and Monogram models can use different parts. The technician checks parts availability before the visit.

### Are you an authorized GE service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by GE Appliances. If your appliance is under the GE warranty, contact GE Appliances first.

### My GE oven shows an F code. What does that mean?

GE ovens use F codes to flag faults, often in a sensor, the door lock or the control. The exact meaning varies by model. Write the code down and give it to us when you call.

### Where is the model number on my GE range?

Open the oven door and check the front frame, or pull out the storage drawer and look on the frame. On some models it is under the cooktop. GE model numbers start with a letter.

### Can you repair my GE appliance the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Common GE parts can often be replaced on the first visit, while some need to be ordered.

### How much does GE appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number ready.
