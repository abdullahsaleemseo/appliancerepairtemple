With the population growing, Troy has added newer houses with the appliance package that came with the build. If an appliance is less than a year or two old, check the manufacturer’s warranty before you book, since independent repair may not be covered.

### Farm and ranch properties

Homes outside town often have a long driveway, a gate and a shop or barn with a spare refrigerator or a chest freezer. Tell us about gates, dogs and where the appliance is when you book. It saves time when the technician arrives.

### Older farmhouses

Older homes can have small kitchens, original water shutoffs and long dryer vent runs. On laundry calls the technician checks the vent and the hookups as well, because they cause many repeat failures.

### Heavy-use laundry

Work clothes, mud and dust are hard on a washer and dryer. Shake off dirt before a load, clean the lint screen every time and do not overload the drum. Those habits keep belts, pumps and bearings going longer.

## Repair or replace? How to decide.

Start with age. Most refrigerators, washers, dryers and ranges are built to last around 10 to 15 years, and dishwashers a little less. A failed part on a five-year-old appliance is almost always worth fixing. On an appliance near the end of that range, it depends on what failed.

Small parts like a belt, a relay, a fan motor, a door switch or a heating element are usually reasonable repairs at any age. A failed sealed system on an old refrigerator or a cracked tub on an old washer usually is not. A common rule of thumb: if the repair would cost more than half of what a comparable new appliance costs, replacement deserves a serious look.

The technician will tell you plainly which side of that line your appliance is on. You make the call.

## Repairs we handle in and around Troy.

Refrigerators that run but do not cool, frost on the freezer wall and ice makers that quit are the most common kitchen calls. Fan motors, defrost parts and water valves are frequent fixes. Chest freezers that hum and click off often have a bad start relay.

Washers that will not drain or spin and dryers that run without heat come next. On every dryer call we check the vent, since a long or clogged vent is one of the most common reasons a dryer stops heating.

Dishwashers that leave dishes dirty or water in the tub, and ovens with a burned bake element or a temperature that has drifted, round out the list.

### Do you cover homes outside Troy city limits?

### Can you come to Troy the same day?

Often. Troy is a short drive from Temple. Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand.

### Is my 12-year-old refrigerator worth fixing?

It depends on what failed. A fan, relay or defrost part is usually worth it. A sealed system failure on an older refrigerator usually is not. The technician will explain the options.

### Do you repair freezers and fridges in shops and barns?

Yes. Make sure the technician can reach the back of the unit and the outlet, and let us know about any gates.

### My appliance is still under warranty. Should I call you?

Contact the manufacturer first, since independent repair may not be covered by a factory warranty. If the warranty has expired or you are not sure, call us with the model number.

### Which appliances do you repair in Troy?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range microwaves, across the major brands.

### What happens if a part has to be ordered?

Many common parts can be replaced on the first visit. Some are model-specific and have to be ordered. The technician will tell you if that is the case and what happens next.

### How much does appliance repair in Troy cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the brand and model number ready.
