## What we see in Little River-Academy.

### Family homes near the schools

Much of town revolves around the Academy schools, and family households run the washer, dryer and dishwasher hard. Thumping, squealing and long dry times are early warnings. A call at that stage often means a smaller repair than waiting for a breakdown.

### Country homes on acreage

Homes outside town often have long driveways, gates and a spare refrigerator or chest freezer in a shop. Tell us about gates, dogs and where the appliance is when you book.

### Older homes in town

Older houses near the center of each community can have small kitchens, original water shutoffs and long dryer vent runs. On laundry calls the technician checks the vent and the hookups, because they cause many repeat failures.

### Appliances that outlast their parts

Plenty of homes here keep a reliable appliance going for years. Belts, door gaskets, relays and heating elements wear out long before the machine itself, and replacing them is usually worth it.

## Stop using it if you notice this.

Most appliance problems can wait for a visit. A few cannot. Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker, and do not use it again until it has been checked, if you see sparks, smell burning plastic or hot wiring, see scorch marks on a cord or outlet, or find water pooling near an outlet or under a machine.

If you smell gas near a range, dryer or water heater, do not switch anything on or off. Leave the house and call your gas provider or 911 from outside. A dryer that feels hot on the outside or smells scorched is also a reason to stop and call, since lint buildup in the vent is a known fire risk.

## What to tell us when you call.

A few details help the technician arrive ready. The brand and model number, usually on a label inside the door, under the lid or on the frame. What the appliance is doing, or not doing, and when it started. Any error code, noise or smell. Anything that happened just before, like a storm, a move or a power outage, and what you have already tried.

You do not need to know what is wrong. A plain description is enough, and the technician will take it from there.

### Can you come to Little River-Academy the same day?

### My dryer smells hot. Should I keep using it?

No. Turn it off, clean the lint screen and check that the outside vent flap opens when the dryer runs. If the smell or heat continues, do not use it until it has been checked.

### Do you repair freezers and fridges in shops?

Yes. Make sure the technician can reach the back of the unit and the outlet, and let us know about any gates.

### My appliance is still under warranty. Should I call you?

Contact the manufacturer first, since independent repair may not be covered by a factory warranty. If the warranty has expired or you are not sure, call us with the model number.

### Which appliances do you repair in Little River-Academy?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range microwaves, across the major brands.

### What happens if a part has to be ordered?
