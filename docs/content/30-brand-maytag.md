## Common Maytag problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Washer will not spin or drain

A clogged drain pump, a worn lid lock or door latch, a broken drive belt or a failed shifter on top-loaders are common causes. The technician checks the drain path and lock before looking at the motor or transmission.

### Dryer runs but no heat

A failed heating element, thermal fuse or high-limit thermostat on electric models, or an igniter or gas valve coils on gas models. A restricted vent is often the root cause, so airflow is checked on every dryer call.

### Refrigerator not cooling

Dusty condenser coils, a failed condenser or evaporator fan, a defrost problem or a start relay come before the compressor. If the compressor has failed, check Maytag’s parts coverage for your model first.

### Dishwasher leaves dishes dirty

A clogged filter, scale in the spray arms, a weak wash pump or an inlet valve that is not letting in enough water. The filter is the first stop.

## Maytag repair questions.

Appliance repair in Temple All brands we service

### Are you an authorized Maytag service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Maytag or Whirlpool Corporation. If your appliance is under Maytag’s warranty, contact Maytag first.

### Does the Maytag 10-year warranty cover my repair?

It depends on the model and the part. The 10-year limited coverage applies to specific parts, such as the washer drive motor and wash basket and some refrigerator compressors, and labor is often not included in the later years. Check your warranty paperwork or ask Maytag.

### Is my older Maytag washer worth repairing?

Often, yes. Pumps, lid locks, belts and suspension parts are reasonable repairs on an older washer. A failed transmission or worn tub bearings usually tip the balance toward replacing it.

### Do Maytag and Whirlpool use the same parts?

Many current models share designs and parts, since both brands belong to Whirlpool Corporation. The full model number tells us exactly which parts fit.

### Can you repair my Maytag the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Some parts are model-specific and may need to be ordered.

### How much does Maytag appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number ready.
