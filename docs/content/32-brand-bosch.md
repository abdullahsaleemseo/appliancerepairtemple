## Common Bosch problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### E15: water in the base

Bosch says E15 means the safety switch has found water in the base and the leak protection has turned on. Turn off the water supply and switch the dishwasher off. The leak usually comes from a hose, a seal, the pump or the door gasket, and it needs to be found and fixed before the unit will run normally.

### E24 or E25: not draining

Drain codes usually point to a blocked filter, a kinked or clogged drain hose, a disposal connection that is blocked, or a jammed or failed drain pump. Clean the filter first. If the code comes back, the pump and hose need to be checked.

### Dishes come out wet

Most Bosch dishwashers dry by condensation rather than with a heated element, so rinse aid matters. If dishes stay wet with rinse aid filled, the technician checks the settings, the rinse aid dispenser and the water heating.

### Refrigerator or range problems

Refrigerators that run warm usually have a fan, defrost or sensor problem. Ranges that will not heat often have a failed element, igniter or control. On induction ranges, the cookware is checked too, since a pan that is not magnetic will not heat.

### Are you an authorized Bosch service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Bosch. If your appliance is under Bosch’s warranty, contact Bosch first.

### What does E15 mean on my Bosch dishwasher?

According to Bosch, E15 means water has been detected in the base and the leak protection system has turned on. Turn off the water supply, switch the dishwasher off and call us. The source of the leak has to be found and fixed.

### My Bosch dishwasher shows E24. What can I check?

Switch it off, then clean the filter at the bottom of the tub and check that the drain hose is not kinked. If the dishwasher drains to a garbage disposal, run the disposal. If the code comes back, the drain pump or hose needs a closer look.

### What are the E-Nr and FD numbers?

The E-Nr is Bosch’s model number and the FD is the production number. Both are on the rating label. Bosch parts often depend on both, so have them ready when you call.
