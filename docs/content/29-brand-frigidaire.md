## Common Frigidaire problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Freezer or fridge running warm

Frost buildup from a defrost problem, a failed evaporator or condenser fan, or a start relay are common causes. On garage freezers, heat and dusty coils make it worse. The technician checks airflow and the defrost system before looking at the sealed system.

### Ice maker not making ice

Often a water inlet valve, a frozen fill tube, a freezer that is not cold enough or the ice maker assembly. Some Frigidaire ice makers have been part of safety recalls, so check your model first (see below).

### Range or oven not heating

A burned bake or broil element on electric models, a weak igniter on gas models, or a temperature sensor that has drifted. Smoothtop burners that will not heat are often a switch or the element itself.

### Dishwasher not draining or cleaning

A clogged filter, a blocked drain hose, a worn drain pump or a weak wash motor. The filter is checked first, since it causes many of these calls.

### Are you an authorized Frigidaire service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Frigidaire or Electrolux. If your appliance is under the manufacturer’s warranty, contact Frigidaire first.

### Has my Frigidaire been recalled?

Some Frigidaire refrigerators, ice makers and ranges have been part of safety recalls. Look up your model and serial number at cpsc.gov or with Frigidaire before paying for a repair, since a recall remedy is handled by the manufacturer.

### Do you repair Frigidaire chest and upright freezers?

Yes, including freezers in garages and shops. Make sure the technician can reach the back of the unit and the outlet.

### Do you work on Frigidaire Gallery and Professional models?

Yes. Give us the full model number so we can check parts, since some Gallery and Professional parts are model-specific.

### Do you repair Kenmore appliances made by Frigidaire?

Yes. Many Kenmore models were built by Frigidaire. Give us the full Kenmore model number and we will check the parts.

### How much does Frigidaire appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number ready.
