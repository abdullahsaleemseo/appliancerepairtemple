# Appliance Repair Tips for Temple homes.

Plain-language guides for the problems we see most in Temple homes: a warm refrigerator, a washer full of water, a dryer running cold, a dishwasher that will not drain. Each one starts with the safe checks you can do yourself and ends with how to tell when it is time to call a technician.

## How to use these guides

- Start with safety. Unplug the appliance, or turn off the breaker, before you look inside or move it.
- Work through the checks in order. Many problems turn out to be a filter, a hose, a setting or a vent.
- Stop when a check needs panels removed, a meter, gas or refrigerant. That is technician work.
