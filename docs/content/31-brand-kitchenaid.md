## Common KitchenAid problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Dishwasher not cleaning or drying

A clogged filter, mineral scale in the spray arms or third-rack jets, a weak wash pump or a failed heating element. Hard water makes scale worse, so the technician checks the filter and spray arms first.

### Dishwasher not draining

Usually a blocked filter, a kinked or clogged drain hose, a garbage disposal knockout plug that was never removed, or a failed drain pump. Each is checked before a part is ordered.

### Refrigerator warm, freezer cold

Often an evaporator fan, a damper or a defrost problem blocking airflow to the fresh-food side. Counter-depth models have tighter spaces, so clean coils and clear vents matter.

### Range slow to heat or uneven

A bake element or igniter on its way out, a temperature sensor that has drifted, or a convection fan that is not running. The technician checks each against the model’s specifications.

### Are you an authorized KitchenAid service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by KitchenAid or Whirlpool Corporation. If your appliance is under KitchenAid’s warranty, contact KitchenAid first.

### Why does my KitchenAid dishwasher leave a white film?

Usually hard water scale or too much detergent. Clean the filter, run a cleaning cycle and check the rinse aid. If the film stays, or dishes come out dirty too, the spray arms or wash pump may need attention.

### Do KitchenAid and Whirlpool use the same parts?

Many core parts are shared, since both brands belong to Whirlpool Corporation. Panels, trim and some controls are KitchenAid-specific. The full model number tells us exactly which parts fit.

### Do you repair KitchenAid stand mixers?

No. We repair major home appliances. For a stand mixer or other countertop appliance, contact KitchenAid.

### Can you repair my KitchenAid the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Some KitchenAid parts are model-specific and may need to be ordered.
