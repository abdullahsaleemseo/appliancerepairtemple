## Common LG problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Refrigerator not cooling

Many LG refrigerators use inverter or linear compressors. Before blaming the compressor, the technician checks the fans, defrost system, sensors and control board, which fail more simply. If it is the compressor, we tell you so you can check the warranty.

### Ice maker slow or frozen

Often a fill tube that has frozen, a water inlet valve, an old filter or the ice maker module. The technician checks each before ordering a part.

### Front-load washer stops with a code

Drain, door lock and unbalanced-load codes are the most common. The pump filter, the lock and the suspension are tested before anything is replaced.

### Dryer runs but no heat

A heating element or thermal fuse on electric models, an igniter or gas valve on gas models. A clogged vent is a common root cause, so airflow is checked too.

## LG repair questions.

### Are you an authorized LG service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by LG. If your appliance is under LG’s warranty, contact LG first.

### My LG refrigerator stopped cooling. Is it the compressor?

Not always. Fans, the defrost system, sensors and the control board can all stop a refrigerator from cooling. The technician tests those first. If the compressor has failed, check whether LG’s compressor coverage applies to your model before paying for that repair.

### Do you repair Kenmore appliances made by LG?

Yes. Some Kenmore models were built by LG. Give us the full Kenmore model number and we will check the parts.

### What does the error code on my LG washer mean?

Codes point to a system, such as draining, the door lock or an unbalanced load. Their meaning varies by model. Note the code and give it to us when you call.

### Can you repair my LG the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Some LG parts are model-specific and may need to be ordered.

### How much does LG appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number and any error code ready.
