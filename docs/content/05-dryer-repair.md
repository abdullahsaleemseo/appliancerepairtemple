## No heat? Electric and gas dryers fail differently

Tell us which one you have when you call. The heating parts are completely different.

### Electric dryers

- Heating element burned open, often from overheating
- Thermal fuse blown by a restricted vent
- High-limit or cycling thermostat failed
- One half of the double 240-volt breaker tripped
- Loose or burned terminal at the power cord block
### Gas dryers

- Igniter glows weakly or not at all
- Flame sensor not switching after the burner lights
- Gas valve coils failed, so the burner lights then goes out
- Thermal fuse blown by a restricted vent
- Gas supply valve partly closed after moving the dryer

Quick answer

## The two-minute airflow check

Do this before you call. It tells you whether the problem is the dryer or the vent.

- 1 Pull out the lint screen and clean it. Shine a flashlight into the slot below it. Packed lint there restricts airflow.
- 2 Look behind the dryer. The hose should be smooth and short, not crushed, kinked or full of sags.
- 3 Run the dryer on high heat and go outside to the vent hood. You should feel strong, warm air and the flap should open fully.
- 4 Weak or no airflow outside means a clogged vent. Strong airflow but no heat means a heating part.
- 5 Call us with what you found, the brand, the model number and whether it is gas or electric.
Do not run a dryer that smells hot or burning while you test. Check the airflow with a load of wet towels only if it has been running normally.

## Dryer problems and what usually causes them

Tap the one that sounds like yours to see the usual cause.

### Runs but no heat

Electric dryers: a burned-out heating element, a blown thermal fuse or a failed high-limit thermostat. Gas dryers: a weak igniter, a bad flame sensor or failed gas valve coils. On electric models, one half of the double breaker can trip and leave the drum turning with no heat.

Elements, fuses, igniters and coils are standard parts.

### Takes two or three cycles to dry

Most of the time this is airflow: a packed lint screen housing, a crushed transition hose or a long vent full of lint. A heating element that has partly failed can do it too.

Fixing the airflow often fixes the dryer.

### Won’t start

Check the breaker first. After that, a bad door switch, start switch, thermal fuse or drive motor can stop a dryer from starting.

Switches and fuses are quick repairs.

### Drum won’t turn

If the motor hums but the drum stays still, the drive belt has usually snapped. A seized idler pulley or drum roller can do it too.

Belt replacement is one of the most common dryer repairs.

### Squealing, thumping or grinding

Squeals usually come from worn drum rollers or the idler pulley. A rhythmic thump often means a flat spot on a roller. Grinding can be worn drum glides or something caught in the blower.

Roller and pulley kits bring back a quiet dryer.

### Gets very hot or smells like burning

Stop using it. Overheating usually comes from lint built up inside the cabinet or the vent, or a cycling thermostat that has failed.

Fixable, but it needs a look before the next load.

### Shuts off early or clothes still wet on auto dry

Auto-dry cycles use moisture sensor bars inside the drum. Dryer sheet residue coats them and fools the dryer into stopping early. A failing thermostat or control board can do the same.

Cleaning the sensor bars fixes many of these. Sensors and thermostats are replaceable.

## Dryer vents: the hidden cause behind many dryer calls

A dryer can only dry as fast as it can push hot, wet air outside. When the vent is restricted, drying times stretch, the cabinet runs hot, and safety parts such as the thermal fuse start to fail. Replacing those parts without clearing the vent only buys time.

The International Residential Code generally limits a dryer exhaust duct to 35 feet, reduced for every elbow, unless the dryer manufacturer allows more. Many laundry rooms in Temple homes vent through an interior wall, into the attic or up to the roof, and those longer runs clog faster.

The short hose behind the dryer matters too. Thin foil or vinyl hoses crush easily and trap lint in their ridges. A semi-rigid or rigid metal transition duct, kept as short and straight as the space allows, moves air better.

## Is your dryer worth repairing?

Dryers are simple machines, and most of their parts are easy to replace. Heating, belt, roller and switch repairs usually make sense even on a dryer that is ten years old. Replacement starts to win when the drum, motor and control board are all worn out at once.

Before you replace a dryer for slow drying, have the vent checked. A new dryer on a clogged vent will dry just as slowly.

- No heat or a broken belt: almost always worth repairing
- Control board failure on a basic 12+ year old dryer: compare against a new one
- Slow drying: check the vent before you decide anything

## Dryer repair questions

### Why does my dryer keep blowing thermal fuses?

A thermal fuse blows when the dryer runs too hot, and the most common reason is a restricted vent. Replacing the fuse without fixing the airflow means it will blow again, so we check the vent on every no-heat call.

### Do you repair gas dryers?

Yes. The technician checks the igniter, flame sensor and gas valve coils. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside before anything else.

### How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

At least once a year for most homes, and more often for large households or long vent runs. If drying times are creeping up, the vent is the first thing to check.

### My dryer is squeaking. Is it about to break?

Usually it means worn drum rollers or an idler pulley. It will not fail right away, but the noise gets worse, and a worn idler can eventually break the belt.

### Can my dryer be fixed the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Many dryer parts are common enough to replace on the first visit. Some model-specific parts have to be ordered.

### How much does dryer repair cost?

It depends on the dryer and the part that failed, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs. Have the brand, the model number and whether it is gas or electric ready.
