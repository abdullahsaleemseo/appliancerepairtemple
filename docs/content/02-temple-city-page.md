Patterns we see across Temple, and what they usually mean for your appliance.

- **Refrigerators and garage freezers working through the summer.** Temple summers are long and hot. A fridge in a warm kitchen or a freezer in the garage runs harder, so a weak start relay, dusty coils or a tired fan motor tends to give out in July and August. - **Hard water buildup in dishwashers, washers and ice makers.** Many Central Texas homes have hard water. Minerals coat dishwasher spray arms, clog washer inlet screens and slow the water feeding an ice maker. It looks like a bad part: cloudy dishes, slow fills, no ice. Sometimes cleaning fixes it. - **Dryer vents in older Temple homes.** Older houses around the Historic District, Midway and Western Hills often have long vent runs or tight laundry closets. - **Newer appliances in West Temple subdivisions.** Newer homes along West Adams Avenue and Highway 317 often have French door refrigerators, front-load washers and smart ranges that report faults as error codes. - **Ovens and ranges that run hot, cold or not at all.** Bake elements burn out, gas igniters weaken and temperature sensors drift. You notice when dinner takes twice as long. We test the element, igniter and sensor before replacing anything. → Oven and stove repair in Temple

### H2: Should you repair it or replace it?

Age and the type of failure matter more than anything else. A newer appliance with one failed part is almost always worth fixing. An older one with a major failure deserves a closer look.

| Appliance | Typical life | Usually worth repairing | Take a closer look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refrigerator | 10 to 15 years | Fans, relays, defrost parts, valves | Compressor or sealed system on an older unit |
| Freezer | 10 to 20 years | Relays, thermostats, seals, fans | Compressor on a small or older chest freezer |
| Washer | 10 to 13 years | Pumps, lid locks, belts, suspension | Transmission or tub bearings |
| Dryer | 10 to 13 years | Elements, igniters, belts, rollers | Drum, motor and board all worn at once |
| Dishwasher | 9 to 12 years | Pumps, inlet valves, latches, seals | Rusted tub or racks plus a failed board |
| Range or oven | 13 to 15 years | Elements, igniters, sensors, switches | Cracked glass top or discontinued board |

Lifespans vary with brand, use and care, so treat these as a guide. Ask three questions: How old is it? Is the rest of it in good shape? Can you still get the part? Built-in refrigerators and wall ovens are usually worth repairing even when older, because replacing them means cabinet and installation work too.

## 5. FAQs (FAQPage markup, visible on the page)

1. **Do you cover all of Temple, Texas?** Yes. We serve every Temple ZIP code: 76501, 76502, 76503, 76504 and 76508. That includes central Temple, the Historic District, West Temple along West Adams Avenue and Highway 317, and the south side of town.
2. **Can a technician come out today?** Often, yes. Same-day appointments are available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Our typical response is about 60 minutes during business hours, which is a typical figure, not a guaranteed arrival time.
3. **What should I do while I wait for the technician?** For a warm refrigerator or freezer, keep the doors closed. For a washer or dishwasher full of water, turn it off and unplug it. For a dryer that smells hot, stop using it. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside before anything else.
4. **Do you repair appliances in rental homes and apartments?** Yes. Tenants should check the lease and tell the landlord first, since the landlord is often responsible for appliances they provide. Landlords and property managers can book directly. Just let us know who approves the repair and who will let the technician in.
5. **My appliance is still under warranty. Should I call you?** Check with the manufacturer first. Independent repair may not be covered by a factory warranty. If the warranty has run out, or you are not sure, call us with the model number and we can talk it through.
6. **Do you work on refrigerators and freezers in the garage?** Yes. Garage units work hard in a Temple summer, and they are a common call. Give the unit some space for airflow and keep the condenser coils clean between visits.
7. **How do I know if a repair is worth it?** Look at the age of the appliance, the condition of the rest of it and whether the part is still available. The technician will explain what failed and whether the repair makes sense, and you make the call.
