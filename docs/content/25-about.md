# About Appliance Repair Temple, your local repair service.

Appliance Repair Temple is an in-home appliance repair service based in Temple, TX. We fix the appliances a household runs on every day: refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges and microwaves, in Temple and the Central Texas towns around it.

Call (254) 555-0147 Request Service

How we think

## Find the real problem. Fix only that.

When an appliance quits, you want two things: someone who shows up when they said they would, and a straight answer about what is wrong. That is the whole idea behind how we work.

Most repairs come down to one failed part. A careful diagnosis finds it, so you are not paying for parts you did not need. The technician explains what failed in plain words, tells you what the repair involves, and starts only once you agree. If the appliance is old enough, or the failure big enough, that a repair does not make sense, you will hear that too.

We stay close to Temple on purpose. Working Bell County and the towns just outside it keeps drive times shorter and scheduling realistic, which matters when your fridge is warm or the washer is full of water.

FAQ

## About us: questions.

### Is Appliance Repair Temple affiliated with any appliance brand?

No. We are an independent repair service, not affiliated with or authorized by any appliance manufacturer. Brand names on this site only describe the appliances we work on.

### Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed and insured.

### Where are you based?

In Temple, TX. Repairs happen in your home. We cover Temple and nearby towns in Bell County and just beyond.

### What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

### How quickly can you come out?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Our typical response is about 60 minutes during business hours, which is a typical figure and not a guaranteed arrival time.

### Why are there no prices on the site?

The cost depends on the appliance, the model and the part that failed, so a number on a web page would only be a guess. Call to discuss your repair needs. Flexible payment options are available.
