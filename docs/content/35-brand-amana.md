## Common Amana problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Washer will not agitate or spin

On top-load agitator washers, a worn lid switch or lid lock, a failed drive coupler, worn agitator dogs or a broken belt are common, and most are reasonable repairs. A washer that hums but does not move often has a jammed pump or a failed motor coupling.

### Dryer runs but no heat

A failed heating element, thermal fuse or thermostat on electric models, or an igniter or gas valve coils on gas models. Clogged vents are a common root cause, especially in apartments with long vent runs.

### Top-freezer refrigerator warm

If the freezer is cold but the fresh-food section is warm, the evaporator fan or the defrost system is the usual suspect. If both are warm, the technician checks the condenser fan, the coils and the start relay.

### Range burner or oven not heating

A burned coil element or a loose receptacle on coil tops, a failed surface element or switch on smoothtops, and a bake element or igniter in the oven. These are common parts on most Amana ranges.

## Amana repair questions.

### Are you an authorized Amana service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Amana or Whirlpool Corporation. If your appliance is under Amana’s warranty, contact Amana first.

### Do you repair Amana air conditioners or furnaces?

No. Amana heating and air conditioning equipment is a separate business from Amana home appliances, and we do not work on HVAC. We repair Amana washers, dryers, refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers and microwaves.

### Is my Amana washer worth repairing?

Usually, yes. Lid switches, couplers, belts and pumps are common, reasonable repairs. A failed transmission on an older washer is the main case where replacing it makes more sense.

### I rent. Can I book the repair myself?

Check your lease first. Many leases make the landlord responsible for the appliances they supply. If your landlord asks you to arrange the visit, let us know who is approving the work.

### Can you repair my Amana the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Common Amana parts are often available quickly; some may need to be ordered.

### How much does Amana appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number ready.
