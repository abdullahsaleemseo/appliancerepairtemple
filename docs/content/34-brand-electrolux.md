## Common Electrolux problems and what they usually mean.

General patterns, not model-specific defects. The technician confirms the cause on your appliance before replacing anything.

### Front-load washer will not drain or unlock

A clogged pump filter behind the small front access door is the most common cause, followed by a failed drain pump or door lock. Clean the filter first; if the washer still will not drain or release the door, call us.

### Washer smells or leaves residue

Front-loaders hold moisture in the door gasket and dispenser. Too much detergent, or the wrong kind, makes it worse. Wipe the gasket, leave the door open after loads and run a cleaning cycle. If water is leaking, the gasket or a hose may need replacing.

### Dryer takes too long or runs cold

A restricted vent is the first suspect, then the heating element, thermal fuse or thermostats on electric models, or the igniter and gas valve coils on gas models. Moisture sensors that are coated with residue can also end cycles early or late.

### Refrigerator or freezer running warm

Fans, the defrost system and dusty condenser coils come before the compressor. Electrolux was part of a 2022 ice maker recall alongside Frigidaire, so check your model at cpsc.gov if the ice maker is involved.

## Electrolux repair questions.

### Are you an authorized Electrolux service provider?

No. Appliance Repair Temple is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with or authorized by Electrolux or Frigidaire. If your appliance is under Electrolux’s warranty, contact Electrolux first.

### Are Electrolux and Frigidaire parts the same?

Many are, since Electrolux owns Frigidaire and the two share platforms. Some trim, controls and features are Electrolux-specific. The full model number tells us exactly which parts fit.

### My Electrolux front-load washer will not drain. What can I check?

Switch it off, put a towel down and open the small access door at the bottom front. Drain the water through the hose if there is one, then clean the pump filter. If it still will not drain, the pump or a hose needs a closer look.

### What is the PNC on my Electrolux label?

The product number code is an identifier Electrolux uses for some models and their parts. If your label has one, include it with the model and serial number when you call.

### Can you repair my Electrolux the same day?

Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Some Electrolux parts are model-specific and may need to be ordered.

### How much does Electrolux appliance repair cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the model number ready.
