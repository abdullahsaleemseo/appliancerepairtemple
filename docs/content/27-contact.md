## Have these handy.

You do not need to know what is wrong. A plain description is enough. These details just help the technician come prepared.

- Your address or ZIP code
- Which appliance it is and the brand
- The model number, if you can find it (inside the door, under the lid or on the frame)
- What it is doing, or not doing, and when it started
- Any error code, noise or smell
- Whether you own the home or rent it
What happens next

Urgent?

## Safety comes before the call.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas provider or 911 from outside. Do not switch lights or appliances on or off first.

If you see sparks or smoke, smell burning, or find water pooling near an outlet, switch off the breaker for that appliance if you can do it safely, and do not use the appliance until it has been checked. For a leak, close the water supply valve behind the appliance. Then call us.

FAQ

## Getting in touch.

### What is the fastest way to reach you?

A phone call during business hours. You talk to a person and can usually settle on a visit window in the same call.

### What happens after I send the request form?

We call you back to go over the problem and confirm a time. The form requests a callback; it does not book an appointment by itself.

### Can I contact you outside business hours?

You can send the request form any time. Our hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and we follow up during those hours.

### Do you have a shop I can bring an appliance to?

Repairs are done in your home. The technician comes to you.
