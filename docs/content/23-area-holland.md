## What that noise usually means.

A refrigerator that clicks every few minutes and never starts often has a failing start relay. A loud buzz or squeal from inside the freezer often points to the evaporator fan, and a rattle from the bottom back can be the condenser fan or a loose drain pan.

A washer that grinds or roars on spin often has worn tub bearings, while a loud thump usually means an unbalanced load or worn suspension. A dryer that squeaks or thumps as it tumbles usually needs new rollers, a belt or an idler pulley. A dishwasher that hums but does not wash may have a jammed or failed pump.

Noises are a clue, not a diagnosis. Note when the sound happens and what the appliance was doing, and tell us when you call.

## Water on the floor? Where to look first.

Under a refrigerator, a leak is often a clogged defrost drain, a cracked water line to the ice maker or a loose water valve fitting. Under a washer, check the hoses at the back and the drain hose connection before anything else. Under a dishwasher, the cause is often a worn door seal, a loose drain connection or suds from the wrong detergent.

If water is pooling near an outlet or cord, switch off the breaker before you touch anything. Close the water supply valve for the appliance if you can reach it, dry the area, and call us.

## Repair in Holland: questions.

### Do you cover homes outside Holland?

Yes, across the 76534 ZIP code, including country roads around town. Part of the ZIP crosses into Milam County, so give us your address and we will confirm.

### Can you come to Holland the same day?

Sometimes. Same-day service is available, subject to technician availability, location, and current demand. Holland is at the south end of our routes, so calling early helps.

### My refrigerator is clicking but not cooling. What is wrong?

A click every few minutes often points to a failing start relay or a compressor that cannot start. Unplug it, check that the coils are clean and the fan turns, then call us if it still will not run.

### My washer is leaking. Should I keep using it?

No. Turn off the water supply to the washer, check the hoses at the back, and call us. Running a leaking washer can damage the floor and anything nearby.

### Do you repair freezers and fridges in shops and barns?

Yes. Make sure the technician can reach the back of the unit and the outlet, and let us know about any gates.

### Which appliances do you repair in Holland?

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, ranges, cooktops and over-the-range microwaves, across the major brands.

### What happens if a part has to be ordered?

Many common parts can be replaced on the first visit. Some are model-specific and have to be ordered. The technician will tell you if that is the case and what happens next.

### How much does appliance repair in Holland cost?

It depends on the appliance and the part, so we do not list prices online. Call to discuss your repair needs with the brand and model number ready.
