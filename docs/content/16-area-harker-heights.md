## What we see in Harker Heights.

### Newer homes, newer appliances

Many Harker Heights homes came with French door refrigerators, front-load laundry pairs and ranges with touch controls. These report problems as error codes. Write the code down, along with the model number, before you call. It helps the technician come prepared.

### Warranty check first

If your appliance is less than a year old, or you bought an extended plan, check that coverage before you book. Independent repair may not be covered by a factory warranty. If the warranty has run out or you are not sure, call us with the model number.

### Larger homes toward the lake

Homes on the south side, toward Stillhouse Hollow Lake and Dana Peak Park, tend to be larger, often with a second refrigerator or a freezer in the garage. A garage unit works harder in summer heat, so clean coils and room to breathe matter more there.

### Rentals and military moves

Being close to Fort Hood, Harker Heights also has plenty of rentals and families on PCS moves. If you rent, check your lease and talk to your landlord before booking, since many leases make the landlord responsible for the appliances they supply.

## Error codes and what to do with them.

An error code points to the system that tripped, not always to the part that failed. A drain code on a front-load washer can mean a clogged pump filter, a kinked hose or a failed pump. A code on a refrigerator display can come from a sensor, a fan or a defrost problem.

Before you call, try the simple steps in your owner’s manual, like clearing the washer’s pump filter or switching the appliance off at the breaker for a minute. If the code comes back, stop resetting it and book a visit. Repeated resets can hide a fault that keeps getting worse.

## Repairs we handle in Harker Heights.

Refrigerator calls lead in summer: warm fresh food sections, frost on the back wall of the freezer, ice makers that stop and water dispensers that leak. Fan motors, defrost parts and water valves are frequent fixes.

Front-load washers bring drain errors, door locks that will not release and musty smells from the door seal. Dryers that run without heat or take two cycles to dry often have a failed heating part or a restricted vent, so we check the airflow on every dryer call.

Dishwashers that leave a film on glasses or water in the tub, and ranges with a dead bake element, a weak igniter or a touch panel that stopped responding, round out the list.
