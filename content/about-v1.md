# About Appliance Repair Temple

Appliance Repair Temple is a local appliance repair company working across Temple and nearby Bell County, fixing refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ranges and microwaves in people's homes. Before you let anyone into your kitchen or laundry room, you should know who's coming, what they're qualified to do and what the visit will involve. This page answers those questions plainly. If you'd rather ask them out loud, call (254) 615-2088.

## Who comes out?

Appliance repair technicians whose routes run through Temple, Belton, Killeen and the smaller towns in between. They diagnose the problem at your home, explain what failed and what the fix involves, and do the repair once you've agreed to it.

What they know best is the houses here. Older homes near downtown with original shutoff valves and long dryer vents. Newer subdivisions with app-connected appliances that report codes. That local knowledge shapes the questions you'll be asked on the phone and what the technician checks first.

## Are you licensed? Are you insured?

Here's how credentials work for appliance repair in Texas, so you can judge any technician, including ours:

- **No general state license.** Texas doesn't issue a general license for household appliance repair. A company that says it's "state licensed" for fixing washers should be able to tell you exactly which license it means.
- **Refrigerant work needs EPA certification.** Federal rules require EPA Section 608 certification for anyone who opens a sealed refrigeration system, such as a refrigerator or freezer compressor repair. Ask to see it for that kind of job.
- **Gas line work belongs to licensed trades.** Connecting or altering gas piping is work for a licensed plumber, or for propane systems, a company licensed for LP gas work. Swapping the appliance end of a connector is different from running new gas line.
- **Insurance is worth asking about.** Liability insurance protects you if something goes wrong in your home. You can ask any technician for proof of current coverage before work starts, and you should.

If any of this matters for your repair, ask on the call. A straight question deserves a straight answer.

## What happens when you call?

The call is short and practical. Have these ready:

1. **The appliance and its brand,** plus the model number from the tag if you can reach it. A phone photo works.
2. **What it's doing,** in a sentence: won't cool, won't drain, clicking, a code on the display.
3. **Your street and ZIP,** so the scheduler knows which route your home falls on. The towns covered are listed on [the coverage map for Temple and Bell County](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/service-areas/).

From there you'll hear how the visit works, what it costs to come out and when the schedule has an opening. If the problem sounds like something you can check yourself first, like a tripped breaker or a clogged filter, you'll hear that too. Prefer to start online? You can [request a repair visit](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/get-connected/) and the details get picked up from there.

## What should I ask any technician before work starts?

Whoever you hire, these questions protect you:

- **The visit fee.** What does it cost to come out and diagnose the problem?
- **The price before work.** Will parts and labor be quoted as one figure before anything is replaced?
- **Parts warranty.** Is the part covered, and for how long?
- **Labor warranty.** If the same problem comes back, is the return visit covered?
- **Timing.** When can someone come, and if a part has to be ordered, how long will it take?

A good technician answers all five without hesitation. Write the answers down.

## Are you tied to any appliance brand?

No. Repairs here are independent of every manufacturer. Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Frigidaire and the rest are named here only to describe the appliances people own; you'll find which brands are covered, and who actually builds them, on [the appliance brands we work on](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/brands/). If your appliance is still under the maker's warranty, contact the maker first.
