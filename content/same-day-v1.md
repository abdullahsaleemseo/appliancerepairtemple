## What to do in the first 15 minutes

These steps buy time without opening a sealed system, bypassing a safety switch or touching a gas line.

1. **Refrigerator or freezer warming.** Keep the doors closed. Put an appliance thermometer on a middle shelf; the fresh-food side should read 40°F or lower, the freezer 0°F.
2. **Washer leaking.** Turn the hot and cold valves on the wall behind the machine clockwise until they stop. Unplug it if the plug is dry and easy to reach; otherwise switch off its breaker. Then towels or a wet-dry vac. The [washer leak and flood repair](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/washer-repair-temple-tx/) page explains the usual causes.
3. **Dishwasher leaking.** Switch off its breaker, close the small valve under the sink and keep the door shut so the water inside stays put.
4. **Oven or range dead.** Look at the breaker panel first. An electric range runs on a double breaker, and when only half of it trips, the clock can glow while the oven stays cold. On a gas range, a gas smell overrides everything else; get out and call from outside. More on [gas range and oven repair](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/oven-stove-repair-temple-tx/).
5. **Anything smoking or sparking.** Kill the power at the panel rather than at the appliance's own switch, and leave the cabinet closed.

A tripped breaker usually rests in the middle, between ON and OFF. Push it fully to OFF, then back to ON. If it snaps off again right away, leave it off. Kitchen, laundry and garage outlets may also have a GFCI reset button that pops out.

## FAQ

### Should I unplug the refrigerator while I wait for the technician?

Usually not. Leave it plugged in unless it smells burnt, sparks or keeps tripping the breaker. A running refrigerator shows the technician which parts work and which don't. Keep the doors closed and let a thermometer do the checking.

### Is it safe to run the dishwasher again after it leaked once?

Not until the cause is known. One leak can come from the wrong detergent or a flipped bowl blocking the spray arm, but a failing pump, hose or door seal will leak again, sometimes under the floor. If you must run it, use a short cycle and stay nearby with towels down.

### Can someone else let the technician in?

Yes, as long as an adult can meet the technician and show where the appliance is. Leave them the model number and a note on what happened. Tell the scheduler who will be home so the arrival call goes to the right phone.

### What should I do with food from a warm refrigerator?

Throw out meat, poultry, seafood, milk, eggs and leftovers that sat above 40°F for more than 2 hours, per USDA guidance. Frozen food that still has ice crystals or reads 40°F or below can be refrozen. When in doubt, throw it out.
