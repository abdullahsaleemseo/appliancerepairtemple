## Maytag Bravos repair: lid lock, shifter and suspension

Bravos top-loaders account for a lot of Maytag washer repair in Temple. The lid lock has to confirm the lid is closed before the washer will agitate or spin, and a worn one leaves the machine filled and stalled. The shift actuator, a small motor-driven part on the bottom of the washer, switches the drive between wash and spin; when it fails, the washer may hum, click or spin without washing. Worn suspension rods let the tub drift so it knocks the cabinet on high-speed spin, even with a balanced load.

A Maytag Bravos repair is usually one of those three parts, and each is a routine repair on a machine of reasonable age. ## Maytag dryers: fuse, belt and rollers

For Maytag dryer repair, the thermal fuse is the common no-heat or no-start culprit, and it usually blows because airflow is restricted. Replacing it without checking the vent tends to end with another blown fuse, which is why a [dryer that's stopped heating](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dryer-repair-temple-tx/) gets its airflow checked first. A broken belt leaves the motor running while the drum sits still, and worn rollers or an idler pulley announce themselves with a thump or squeal that gets worse over months.

## How to read a Maytag model number

The first letters of a Maytag model number name the product. MVW is a top-load washer (including the Bravos line), MHW a front-load washer, and MED and MGD are electric and gas dryers. Kitchen models follow the same idea, such as MFI for a French door refrigerator with an in-door ice maker. ## Maytag repair FAQ

### Is a Maytag washer the same as a Whirlpool inside?

Many current models share the same platform, drive parts and controls, since Whirlpool owns Maytag. Maytag versions often add heavier-duty features or different styling, and sometimes longer parts coverage. Match parts by your exact model number rather than assuming a Whirlpool part fits.

### Why does my Maytag Bravos click but not agitate?

A clicking Bravos that fills but won't wash often has a failing shift actuator or a lid lock that isn't confirming closed. Unplug the washer for a minute to reset it, then try a short cycle. If it keeps clicking, the actuator or lid lock needs testing.

### Can I stack a Maytag front-load washer and dryer?

Many Maytag front-load pairs can be stacked with the stacking kit made for that model. Check the manual for the exact kit and confirm the dryer's vent connection and the washer's drain still reach once stacked. Don't stack top-load models.

### Why does my Maytag dryer squeak when it starts?

A squeak at startup that fades or changes as the drum turns usually comes from the idler pulley or drum rollers drying out and wearing. It won't fix itself, and a seized pulley can eventually break the belt, so have the belt path checked before that happens.
