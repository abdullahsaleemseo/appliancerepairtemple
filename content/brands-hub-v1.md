Why it matters: a part, a diagnostic mode or a known weak spot often carries across a whole family. A lid lock that fails on a Whirlpool top-loader may be the same part in a Maytag from the same years, and an older Kenmore fridge may really be a Frigidaire or an LG underneath. That's why the model number, not the badge, decides which parts and fixes apply.

## Brands FAQ

### Do you repair all appliance brands?

Yes for the major names in Temple homes: the twelve brands linked on this page, plus their sister lines like GE Profile, Café and Frigidaire Gallery. For anything less common, phone with the label details and ask whether a technician can take it on.

### Do you fix high-end appliances like Sub-Zero or Viking?

It depends on the brand, the model and parts availability. High-end and built-in appliances often use specialized parts and installation, and some brands restrict who can buy those parts. Call with the brand and full model number before booking, and ask directly whether that appliance can be serviced.

### Where do I find my appliance's model number?

Look for a sticker or metal plate: inside the fridge cabinet, around the door opening on dryers and front-loaders, under the lid of a top-loader, along a dishwasher's door edge, and on a range's frame once the bottom drawer is out. Snap a picture of it so nothing gets misread.
