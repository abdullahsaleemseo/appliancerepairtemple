## Summer: garage fridges and freezers

A refrigerator in a hot garage is working against the room all day. When it starts losing the fight, the first sign is usually a fresh-food side that won't drop below the mid-40s while the freezer still feels cold. Dust on the coils and a slow condenser fan are the common causes, and both are worth checking before anything else. If the coils are clean and it's still warm, the defrost system and controls come next, laid out in [the cooling checks for a refrigerator that runs warm](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/refrigerator-repair-temple-tx/).

## Fall: deer season and the deep freezer

Loading a chest freezer with a whole season's meat in one afternoon asks a lot of it. A freezer can only pull heat out so fast, and a big warm load can take a day or more to come down to temperature, sometimes thawing what was already in there. Freezing in batches helps. So does a pre-season defrost: an inch of frost on the walls insulates the food from the cold. If the freezer runs constantly, frosts unevenly, or won't get below the teens, work through the causes listed under [freezer repair, chest and upright](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/freezer-repair-temple-tx/).

## Winter: cold snaps and unheated rooms

A washer in an unheated room can freeze water left in its pump and hoses, and a cracked fill hose won't show itself until the next load floods the floor. On nights headed below freezing, close the supply valves, disconnect and drain the fill hoses, and run a short spin to clear the pump. A garage refrigerator has a different winter problem: its thermostat sits in the fresh-food section, so when the garage itself is cold, the compressor barely runs and the freezer side can start to soften.

## Spring: dryer vents

Spring pollen coats the outside vent hood, and birds like a warm vent opening for nesting. Either one restricts airflow, and a restricted dryer runs hot, takes longer and blows thermal fuses. Go outside while the dryer runs and look: the flap should open and you should feel strong airflow. If it's weak, a full [dryer vent cleaning from dryer to roof or wall](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dryer-vent-cleaning-temple-tx/) clears the whole run, not just the end you can see.

### Do you come out to 76554?

### Should I defrost my chest freezer before deer season?

If there's more than about a quarter inch of frost on the walls, yes. Move the food to coolers, unplug the freezer, let it thaw with the lid open and towels down, dry it, and give it several hours to cool back down before loading.

### How do I keep a washer in an unheated room from freezing?

On nights below freezing, close both water valves, disconnect and drain the fill hoses, and run a short drain or spin cycle to empty the pump. Reconnect everything once the room warms up.
