## Bosch error codes worth knowing: E24 and E25

Two codes come up more than any others on Bosch dishwasher repair calls in Temple:

- **E24:** water isn't draining as it should, usually because the drain hose is kinked, crushed behind the dishwasher or blocked, or the connection at the sink or garbage disposal is clogged.
- **E25:** the drain pump itself is blocked, often by a bit of glass, a seed or a label caught under the pump cover in the sump.

For E24, check the hose under the sink first; a new disposal with its knockout plug still in place causes it too, and [what to check on a disposal](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/garbage-disposal-repair-temple-tx/) covers that connection. For E25, lifting out the filter reveals a small pump cover to check for debris. Confirm both codes in your manual, since meanings can vary by model and age.

The E15 leak code, covered alongside [the full water path of a dishwasher](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dishwasher-repair-temple-tx/), deserves a reminder here: the leak behind it, not the code, is what needs fixing.

## Bosch repair FAQ

### Why does my Bosch dishwasher smell when I open it?

Food trapped in the filter or the sump starts to smell between cycles, especially if the dishwasher sits closed for days. Rinse the filter assembly, wipe the door seal, and run a hot empty cycle with a dishwasher cleaner. Leaving the door slightly open after a cycle helps.

### Can I use any rinse aid in a Bosch?

Yes, a standard dishwasher rinse aid works, and Bosch models rely on it more than most because of condensation drying. Keep the dispenser filled, and turn the dosage setting up a step if dishes come out spotty or wet.

### Does a Bosch ventless dryer need a vent or a drain?

It doesn't need an outside vent. It needs either its water container emptied regularly or a drain hose connected to a standpipe or sink, depending on the model and how it's installed. Check the manual for the drain setup and the condenser cleaning schedule.

### Why won't my Bosch compact washer door open after the cycle?

The door stays locked until the drum stops, the water is drained and the lock cools, which can take a minute or two. If water is still inside, the drain pump filter is often clogged. Drain it through the small hatch at the bottom front before calling.
