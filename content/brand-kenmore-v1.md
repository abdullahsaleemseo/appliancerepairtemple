## Kenmore repair FAQ

### My Kenmore model number has no dot. What now?

Look for a separate serial or model plate; some labels list the model number in a different format or split it across lines. Newer Kenmore appliances may use a different numbering style altogether. Photograph the whole label and read every line on the call so it can be matched.

### Can I still get parts for an old Kenmore?

Often, yes, because the parts usually come from the original maker and many stay in production for years. Very old or discontinued models can be harder. Having the full model number, prefix included, is what makes the cross-reference possible.

### Is a Kenmore the same as the brand that built it?

Inside, very often. A Kenmore usually shares its platform, main parts and failure patterns with the maker's own model from the same years, with Kenmore styling and sometimes different features. That's why parts and codes are looked up through the maker.

### Does my old Sears protection agreement still cover my Kenmore?

It depends on the agreement and whether it's still active. Find the paperwork or account details and check its current status before booking a paid repair. If it's no longer valid, the repair decision is the same as for any appliance of that age.
