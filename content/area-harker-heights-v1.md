## Before you call: a quick decision flow

Read down the list and stop at the first line that fits.

- **IF** water is on the floor, **THEN** close the supply valve behind the appliance before anything else. For a dishwasher, that valve is usually under the kitchen sink.
- **IF** the appliance is less than a year old, **THEN** find the receipt and check the manufacturer's warranty. A covered repair goes through the maker.
- **IF** you bought an extended plan from the store, **THEN** check that plan before booking a paid visit.
- **IF** there's a code on the display or an alert in the app, **THEN** write it down or take a screenshot, exactly as shown, letters and dashes included.
- **IF** the app shows the machine offline but it runs fine, **THEN** that's a Wi-Fi or router problem, not a broken appliance. Reconnect it in the app.
- **IF** the display is blank, **THEN** check the breaker and, for kitchen and laundry outlets, any GFCI outlet nearby that may have tripped.
- **IF** none of that applies, or the warranty has ended, **THEN** it's time for a technician.

## What the code and the app can tell you

A code narrows the problem down; it rarely names the exact failed part. A drain code on a front-loader can mean a clogged pump filter you can clear yourself in ten minutes, or a pump that has actually died. Look yours up in the [appliance error code decoder](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/appliance-error-code-decoder/) first, and if it points to something simple, try that before booking.

## Still under warranty? Start with the maker

A refrigerator or washer bought with the house, or replaced in the last year, could still fall inside the maker's coverage. Check the purchase date, or the closing date if it came with the home, and call the maker's service line first. A builder's home warranty sometimes covers appliances for a short period too, so look through the closing paperwork. After that coverage ends, any qualified technician can do the work.

## Harker Heights FAQ

### Is 76548 in the service area?

Yes. All of 76548 is covered, from the Market Heights shopping area to the homes near Stillhouse Hollow Lake. Give your street address when you call so the visit can be placed on the right part of the route.

### My fridge sent an alert to my phone. Should I call right away?

Read the alert first. Door-open, filter and power-outage alerts usually need no repair. An alert that names a temperature problem or an error code is worth a call, especially if the fridge feels warm. Screenshot it before it clears.

### The app says my washer has an error, but the display is blank. What does that mean?

Often the washer lost power mid-cycle and the app is showing the last error it received. Check the breaker and the outlet, then try a short rinse cycle. If it powers up and shows the code again, write it down and call.
