# About Appliance Repair Temple

We repair home appliances across Temple and Bell County: refrigerators and freezers, washers and dryers, dishwashers, ranges, wall ovens, cooktops and microwaves. If a fridge, washer, oven or anything else at home has quit, call us at (254) 615-2088, say what it's doing, and a technician takes it from there.

## Appliance repair for homes, and nothing else

The work happens in people's kitchens, laundry rooms and garages, not in a shop. A technician comes to the house, finds what failed, explains it in plain words and fixes it once you've agreed to the price. That's the whole job, day after day, so the problems you're dealing with are ones a technician has usually seen many times already: the fridge that's cold in the freezer and warm everywhere else, the washer that fills and then just sits there, the oven that clicks but won't light.

Most calls start the same way. Something stopped working, nobody's sure if it's worth fixing, and there's a load of wet towels or a freezer full of food waiting on the answer. The aim is to get you that answer quickly and honestly, including when the honest answer is that a repair doesn't make sense for an appliance that old.

## What Temple homes teach you

Local detail changes the advice more than people expect.

**The housing mix.** Temple has older houses near Downtown Temple with alcoves built for smaller refrigerators and laundry closets with long, crooked dryer vents. It has ranch homes from a few decades back with original shutoff valves, newer subdivisions off West Adams Ave full of app-connected appliances that report error codes, and rentals that change hands every lease. A technician checks the hookups and the space around the appliance as well as the appliance itself, because in an older house that's often where the trouble started.

**Hard water.** Much of the water around here leaves scale behind. It builds up in ice maker and washer inlet valves until the fill slows, and it leaves a white film inside dishwashers. If your ice cubes have been getting smaller for months, that's usually the reason, and the fixes are laid out with the rest of the cooling problems in [what goes wrong with refrigerators and ice makers](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/refrigerator-repair-temple-tx/).

**Summer heat.** A garage fridge or chest freezer in a Central Texas July runs nearly nonstop, and a dryer pushing air through a long vent into a hot attic works harder than the manual ever planned for. Dusty condenser coils and lint-packed vents cause a lot of summer breakdowns that look like failed parts.

## What happens when you call

1. **You describe it.** The appliance, its brand, the model number from the tag when it's reachable, and what it's doing. Any code on the display helps.
2. **You ask your questions.** The visit fee, how the repair price is worked out and the next open slot are all fair to ask before anything is booked.
3. **A technician comes out and finds the cause.** Then comes a plain explanation of the failed part, the fix and the cost.
4. **You decide.** The repair starts once you've said yes. If the numbers don't make sense for the appliance's age, you'll hear that too.

Ready to start with step one? Call (254) 615-2088 with the appliance, the brand, the model number and a sentence about what it's doing.

## Licenses, insurance and what to ask

Texas doesn't issue a general state license for household appliance repair, so "state licensed" on its own doesn't tell you much. A few things do matter:

- **Refrigerant work** needs EPA Section 608 certification. That applies to sealed system repairs on refrigerators and freezers, such as a compressor or a refrigerant leak.
- **Gas line work** belongs to a licensed plumber. Swapping the connector at the back of a range is one thing; running or changing gas piping is another.
- **Insurance** protects you if something goes wrong in your home. Ask any technician, including ours, for proof of coverage.
- **The price** should come before the work. Ask for the full repair cost, parts and labor, before anything is replaced, and ask whether the parts and labor are covered afterward.

The [answers to common repair questions](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/faq/) cover cost, the visit and what comes after it in more detail.
