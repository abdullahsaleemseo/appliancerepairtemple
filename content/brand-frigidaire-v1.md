## Frigidaire, Gallery or Professional?

There are three Frigidaire lines, and the second letter of most current model numbers tells you which one you have: FF for standard Frigidaire, FG for Gallery and FP for Professional. Standard Frigidaire covers the basic, builder-grade models: plain finishes, fewer features and the lowest prices. Gallery is the step up, with stainless finishes, more convenience features and more electronics. Professional is the top line, with heavier styling and more advanced features. Confirm the letters on the tag, since older models use different patterns.

The line changes the repair math. A part on a standard model has to justify itself against a cheaper replacement, while Frigidaire Gallery repair and Professional repairs are worth it more often because replacing those appliances costs more. Many core parts overlap across the three, which helps. If you have an older Kenmore, check the model number too: Kenmore refrigerators starting with 253 and ranges starting with 790 were built by Frigidaire, so the same parts and patterns apply.

## Common Frigidaire refrigerator problems

Airflow and ice account for most Frigidaire refrigerator repair in Temple. On top-freezer and side-by-side models, a damper control lets cold air from the freezer into the fresh-food section; when it sticks, the fridge side warms while the freezer stays cold. A stalled evaporator fan, or ice packed over the coil after a defrost failure, looks exactly the same from the outside. Ice makers on side-by-side models are another frequent call, often from a failed inlet valve or a frozen fill tube. ## Frigidaire repair FAQ

### Are Frigidaire and Electrolux parts the same?

Some are. Both brands belong to the same company, and many parts overlap, especially in refrigerators, ranges and laundry. Electrolux models tend to have more premium features and trim. Match any part by the full model number rather than assuming it fits.

### Why is my Frigidaire fridge freezer cold but the fridge warm?

Most often the damper control between the freezer and fresh-food sections is stuck, or the evaporator fan has stopped, or the coil behind the freezer panel is frosted over. Check that nothing blocks the vents inside both sections, then have the fan, damper and defrost system checked.

### What does the F10 code mean on my Frigidaire oven?

On many Frigidaire ranges, F10 means the oven temperature is higher than it should be, often from a failed temperature sensor or a control fault. Leave the range switched off at the panel until it's checked. Meanings vary by model, so confirm it in your manual.

### Is a Frigidaire Gallery worth more to repair than a standard model?

Generally yes. Gallery appliances cost more to replace, so the same repair is a better value. Many internal parts are shared with standard Frigidaire models, which keeps the parts side reasonable. Weigh the quoted repair against what a new Gallery model of the same size would cost.
