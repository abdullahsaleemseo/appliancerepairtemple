## My LG fridge stopped cooling. Is it the compressor?

Maybe, but not always, and the difference matters. Many LG refrigerators use a linear compressor, a design that runs quietly and efficiently by moving a piston back and forth instead of rotating. Some of those compressors have failed early, and the problem drew a wave of owner complaints and lawsuits.

A proper diagnosis comes first, because the cheaper suspects fail more often: an evaporator or condenser fan, the defrost heater or its sensor, or the control board. LG fridges often name these on the display, with codes such as Er FF for the freezer fan, Er CF for the condenser fan and Er dH for the defrost system on many models. When the compressor itself is the cause, the signs are a fridge that warms in both sections while the compressor runs constantly or clicks without starting, and a technician confirms it by checking the sealed system and the compressor's electrical readings. ## Is my LG still covered, and who pays for the labor?

Start with the warranty card or the registration in your LG account. If the appliance is within its main warranty period, contact LG first, since in-warranty work is LG's to arrange. If only a long-term parts warranty applies, such as on a compressor or a direct-drive motor, LG may cover the part while you pay for the labor and the visit. Ask LG how that works for your model before booking.

That math is why LG owners should get two numbers on any big repair: what the part would cost without coverage, and what the labor costs either way. For repairs outside the warranty, the choice of who does the work is yours. Federal law backs that choice: the FTC's guidance on the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act says a manufacturer generally can't void coverage simply because an independent technician did the work, though it won't pay for damage a bad repair causes.

## My LG dryer shows d80. What does that mean?

Many LG dryers have a feature called Flow Sense that measures airflow through the vent and warns you as it drops. The codes step up with the restriction:

| Code | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| d80 | The vent is about 80% restricted | Plan a vent cleaning soon; drying times are already longer |
| d90 | About 90% restricted | Clean the vent before running many more loads |
| d95 | About 95% restricted | Stop and clear the vent; the dryer is barely moving air |

The dryer usually keeps running, which is exactly why people ignore the code. A badly restricted vent means longer cycles and more wear on heating parts. The fix is almost always in the vent rather than the dryer, and [dryer vent cleaning along the full route](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dryer-vent-cleaning-temple-tx/) matters most for the long attic runs common in Temple homes. If the code returns right after a cleaning, or the dryer won't heat at all, look at [why a dryer stops heating](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dryer-repair-temple-tx/), since a thermal fuse or heater may have failed along the way. ## LG repair FAQ

### Does LG's long compressor warranty cover the repair visit?

Usually not after the first year. Long-term coverage on LG compressors and motors is typically parts-only, so labor, refrigerant and the visit may be yours to pay. Terms differ by model and purchase date, so check your warranty card or ask LG directly before booking.

### Why is my LG fridge display showing Er with letters after it?

That's an error code. The letters name the area at fault on many models, such as FF for the freezer fan or dH for the defrost system. Write the full code down, try a power reset once, and call if it returns.

### Can I keep using my LG dryer while it shows d80?

Yes, but plan a vent cleaning soon. At d80 the dryer still works, just slower and hotter than it should, and each load costs more energy. Don't let it reach d95, where airflow is nearly gone, and never ignore a burning smell.

### Is my LG fridge's ice maker the same problem as Samsung's?

LG uses an in-door ice compartment on some French door models too, and frost in that compartment is a known issue on certain ones. The fix starts with finding why cold air is escaping, not just clearing the ice.
