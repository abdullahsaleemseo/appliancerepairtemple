## Amana top-load washers

Many Amana washers are agitator top-loaders. When one won't spin, the lid switch or lid lock is the first suspect, since the washer refuses to spin if it can't tell the lid is shut. Older direct-drive designs use a small rubber and plastic motor coupler that wears out and breaks, leaving the motor humming with nothing turning. Water left in the tub points to the drain pump or a sock caught in it. ## Amana repair FAQ

### Do you repair Amana air conditioners?

No. Amana heating and air conditioning equipment is a separate product line made by a different company, and it needs a licensed HVAC contractor. Amana refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers and microwaves are household appliances, and those are what this service repairs.

### Are Amana and Whirlpool parts the same?

Often, yes. Amana is a Whirlpool brand, so many of its parts also fit Whirlpool and Maytag models built in the same years. The part still has to be matched to your exact model number, because similar-looking parts can differ.

### My Amana washer fills and agitates but won't spin. What should I check?

Check that the lid closes fully, nothing blocks the switch or lock, and the load isn't lopsided. If it still won't spin, the lid switch, the motor coupler on older direct-drive models, or a drain problem is the likely cause.

### Is it worth fixing a cheap Amana fridge?

For a defrost part, a fan or a thermostat, usually yes, because those repairs cost well under the price of a new fridge. When an older one has a failed compressor or a refrigerant leak, a new basic fridge usually makes more sense.
