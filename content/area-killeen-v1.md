## The appliance move checklist

Most move-related damage is avoidable. Work through this before the truck shows up and again before you plug anything in.

### Moving out

- **Refrigerator:** empty it, unplug it the day before so the freezer defrosts, and leave the doors propped open to dry. Shut off and disconnect the water line if it has an ice maker, and catch the water left in it. Take the shelves and bins out or secure them.
- **Washer:** close both water valves, disconnect the hoses and drain them. If it's a front-loader and you still have the shipping bolts that came with it, put them back in to keep the drum from banging around.
- **Dryer:** clean the lint screen and the vent behind it. For a gas dryer, have the gas shut off and capped by someone qualified; don't just pull the line.
- **Range:** electric ranges unplug; gas ranges need the gas shut off and capped the same way as a dryer.

### Moving in

- **Refrigerator:** let it stand upright before plugging it in, a few hours at least and longer if it rode on its side, so the oil settles back into the compressor. Then give it most of a day to cool before loading food. Toss the first trays of ice it makes.
- **Washer:** take the shipping bolts out before the first load. A front-loader run with the bolts in shakes violently and can be damaged. Use new fill hoses if the old ones are bulging or cracked, level the machine, and check that the drain hose sits in the standpipe without being jammed in tight.
- **Dryer:** check the plug matches the outlet; older houses often have a three-slot dryer outlet while newer ones use four, and the plug must fit it. Use a rigid or semi-rigid metal vent, not foil or plastic, and leave a few inches behind the dryer so it doesn't crush.
- **Range:** make sure the anti-tip bracket is installed, especially with kids in the house.

If something still won't run right after the move, dial (254) 615-2088 and have ready the address, the brand and model number, what happened during the move (laid on its side, bolts left in, new outlet) and what it's doing now. That move history often points straight at the cause.

## Common Killeen repairs

### Washers

New-to-you washers in Killeen rentals tend to show the same few problems: leaks from worn or loosely tightened hoses on the first load, drain trouble from a hose pushed too far into the standpipe, and front-loaders that shake because they're not level or still have bolts in. ### Dryers

A crushed vent after a move is the classic Killeen dryer call. The dryer runs but takes two cycles, gets hot to the touch, or blows its thermal fuse and stops heating. Fix the vent first, then look at the heat parts, the order laid out in [the steps for a dryer that won't heat](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dryer-repair-temple-tx/). A vent run that's long, old or packed with lint from the last tenant is a job for [professional dryer vent cleaning](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/dryer-vent-cleaning-temple-tx/).

### Refrigerators

A fridge that won't cool after a move usually needs time, not parts: it was plugged in too soon, the doors were left ajar, or the vents were blocked by boxes. Give it a day. If it's still warm, the condenser fan, the coils and the defrost system are next, as in [the refrigerator checklist for a warm fridge](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/refrigerator-repair-temple-tx/).

## Killeen FAQ

### I live on post at Fort Hood. Can I book a repair directly?

Check with your housing office or maintenance line first, because the housing program usually covers appliances supplied with the home. If they tell you to arrange it yourself, call with the address and appliance details.

### My fridge rode on its side during the move. Is it ruined?

Usually not. Stand it upright and wait before plugging it in, a few hours at least and longer if it was on its side for a long trip. Then give it most of a day to reach temperature. If it still isn't cooling after that, call.

### The washer in my new rental leaked on the first load. Who do I call?

First, turn off the water to the washer and dry the floor. Then tell your landlord or property manager, since many leases put appliance repairs with them. A leak on the first load is often a loose or worn hose, which the owner may want handled before anything else.

### Is there a travel charge for Killeen?

Ask when you call. Killeen is farther from Temple than some towns, so give your address up front; any travel charge and the visit fee are explained before a visit is booked.
