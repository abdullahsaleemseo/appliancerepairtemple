## GE oven error codes: what the F-codes mean

Ranges from this brand report oven faults as F-codes on the display, and ovens are where GE oven repair calls most often start. These are common on GE ranges and wall ovens:

| Code | What it usually points to |
|---|---|
| F1 | Electronic control fault or runaway temperature |
| F2 | Oven temperature too high |
| F3 | Oven temperature sensor reads as open |
| F4 | Oven temperature sensor reads as shorted |
| F7 | A stuck or shorted keypad or function key |

Meanings can vary by model and age, so confirm yours in the owner's manual before acting on it.

F3 and F4 both lead back to the oven temperature sensor, the thin probe at the back of the oven cavity, or its wiring. That's one of the more straightforward GE oven repairs. An F1 or F2 is more serious: the control or a relay may be letting the oven heat without stopping, so turn the oven off at the breaker until it's checked. Bake elements, igniters and the rest of the range follow the same patterns as any other brand, covered under [oven and stove repair by part](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/oven-stove-repair-temple-tx/).

## GE refrigerators: dispensers, ice and the filter chip

Water and ice cause more GE refrigerator repair in Temple than cooling does. Dispensers that slow down or stop, ice makers that quit and frozen fill tubes are common, and they follow the same water route as any refrigerator ice maker, which [ice maker and water dispenser repair](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/ice-maker-repair-temple-tx/) walks through stop by stop.

One quirk is GE's own. Some newer GE fridges use a water filter with a small RFID chip, and the fridge checks for it. If it doesn't recognize the filter, the dispenser may slow down or stop delivering water at all, even though nothing is broken. Before assuming a failed valve, check that the filter is a type your model accepts and that it's fully seated. Cooling problems, on the other hand, behave like any other fridge, and [why a refrigerator stops cooling](https://appliancerepairtemple.com/refrigerator-repair-temple-tx/) applies to GE models as much as any.

## GE repair FAQ

### Can I reset my GE oven after an F-code?

Turning the breaker off for a minute or two and back on clears some codes. If the same code returns, the reset only hid it. For F1 or F2, keep the oven off at the breaker until it's checked, since the oven may be overheating.

### Are GE Profile and Café parts the same as regular GE parts?

Many internal parts, like sensors, igniters and some valves, overlap across the lines. Trim, handles, glass, and some controls and features are line-specific. Always order by the full model number rather than the brand line alone, or ask the technician to cross-check it.

### Why does my GE fridge show a filter light right after a new filter?

On many models the filter reminder has to be reset by hand after a change, usually by holding a button on the dispenser panel for a few seconds. The exact button varies by model, so check the manual or the label inside the fridge.

### Does it matter that GE Appliances is owned by Haier now?

Not much for repairs. GE Appliances still designs and builds most of its products under the GE brand names, and parts for older GE models remain widely available. Haier also sells products under its own name, which are separate from the GE lines.
