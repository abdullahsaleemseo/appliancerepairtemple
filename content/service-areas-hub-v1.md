# Areas We Serve Around Temple, TX

In-home appliance repair visits cover Temple and the Bell County towns around it: Belton and Salado down I-35, Killeen and Harker Heights out US-190, plus a handful of small towns east and north. If your town is in the table below, call (254) 615-2088 with your street address and ZIP and the scheduler will confirm it before booking. If it isn't listed but you're close, call anyway; plenty of addresses between these towns are covered too.

## How the area breaks down

### Temple itself

Temple is the center of the map, from the older neighborhoods near downtown and the hospital district to the newer subdivisions out past Loop 363 on the west and south sides. Every Temple ZIP is covered, and the town's own repair details live on the homepage linked in the table above.

### I-35 north

Troy is a short drive up I-35 from Temple, small and quiet, with older farmhouses and newer homes on larger lots. Calls from out there are often for well-used laundry pairs and second freezers in the garage or barn.

### I-35 south

Belton is Temple's next-door neighbor and the county seat, close enough that many addresses feel like one town. Farther down the interstate, Salado mixes historic homes near Main Street with newer houses on acreage.

### US-190 west

Head west on US-190, now also signed I-14, and you pass Nolanville, then Harker Heights, then Killeen. This is the busiest stretch of the area: rentals, military families on the move, and a lot of apartments and duplexes with landlord-owned appliances. Renters should clear a repair with the landlord or property manager first, since they usually decide who fixes their appliances.

### East and southeast

East of Temple, US-190 runs out to Rogers through open farm country. To the southeast, TX-95 passes Little River-Academy and then Holland. Homes out here are spread out, so a clear address, a gate code if there is one, and a note on which building the appliance is in all help.

### Lake Belton

Morgan's Point Resort sits on the shore of Lake Belton, west of Temple. Lake homes and weekend places often have extra refrigerators, ice makers and freezers that sit unused for weeks, then get loaded up all at once.

### Northwest

Moody is just over the line in McLennan County, up TX-317. It's one of the farther towns on the list, so mention it when you call and ask about scheduling for that side.

## Before you call

Have three things ready: your street address and ZIP, the appliance and its brand, and a one-line description of what it's doing. A photo of the model tag helps if you can get to it. Then call (254) 615-2088. The scheduler will confirm your address is covered, talk through timing, and answer questions about the visit before anything is booked.

## Do farther towns cost more?

Some addresses farther from Temple may carry a travel charge; the answer turns on your exact location. Ask when you call: give your address first, and you'll hear about any travel charge, along with the visit fee, before a visit is set. Nothing should come as a surprise on the day.

## Service area FAQ

### Is my address covered if my town isn't listed?

Often, yes. The table lists the towns with their own pages, but many rural roads and small communities between them are covered too. Call with your street address and ZIP and you'll get a yes or no on the phone.

### How soon can a technician get to me?

It depends on the day's schedule, where you are and what the repair needs. Same-day visits are often possible, depending on the schedule and parts. Ask when you call.

### Do you cover Fort Hood on-post housing?

On-post housing has its own maintenance system, so residents should contact their housing office first. Off-post homes and rentals in Killeen, Harker Heights and Nolanville are covered like any other address.
